Clipsmoney Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clipsmoney (“we”, “the Service”) handles information when you use the Service to create and publish short-form esports patch videos.

1. Information we collect

  • Account connection data: OAuth tokens and basic profile identifiers needed to publish on your behalf (TikTok and/or YouTube/Google)
  • Content data: scripts, captions, generated videos, QA metadata, and related job records
  • Technical logs: errors, publish status, and basic usage logs needed to operate the Service

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising networks or data brokers.

2. How we use information

  • To generate and store videos you request
  • To authenticate with TikTok / YouTube and publish content you authorize
  • To maintain security, debug failures, and improve reliability

3. Sharing

We share data with third parties only as needed to run the Service — for example, TikTok or Google/YouTube (to publish) and infrastructure providers that host the app or store tokens you configure. We do not sell or rent personal data.

4. Retention

We keep tokens and content as long as needed to provide the Service, or until you disconnect / request deletion. You can revoke platform access in TikTok or Google account settings. Operator deployments typically store secrets in local environment files that are not committed to source control.

5. Your choices

  • Disconnect TikTok / YouTube access at any time
  • Request deletion of stored tokens and generated media by contacting the operator
  • Keep auto-publish disabled and upload only from approved render folders

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: contact the Clipsmoney operator via the GitHub organization esportspatchnotes or the email associated with your TikTok developer app registration.
